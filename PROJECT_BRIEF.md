# Product objective

Produce an informative, brief white-paper-esque account of the state of U.S. trade-facilitation programs, supported by abundant visualizations. The main paper should remain about 2–3 pages; use the visual atlas and inspectable supporting data for detail. Audience: Deputy Ambassador and other senior diplomatic readers. Do not add travel tips.

Explain program ownership, instruments, geographic eligibility, overseas operations and embassy presence, sector/infrastructure interests, and documented country delivery. Compare similarity/dissimilarity only where geographic and sector definitions are comparable. Show gaps and uncertainty visibly.

This GitHub repository is the canonical home for all project materials. Preserve source lineage, dated evidence, data, scripts, methods, validation, historical releases and unresolved records. Future additions should be committed here.

## Evidence discipline

Preserve the existing codebooks and evidence categories. Distinguish recent delivered activity, agency approval/authorization, portfolio records, intake/offer, planned events, historical delivery and unresolved status. Eligibility is not activity; an agency network is not a verified program-to-office assignment. A final-report catalog establishes reported document completion metadata, not construction completion or verified ongoing operations. Missing evidence does not establish inactivity.

Unresolved legacy awards remain visible and must not be upgraded to verified active without primary evidence. Gaps do not halt the whole paper or visualization work. Use explicit statuses, dates, denominators and caveats. Do not manufacture category precision or program ownership.

Priorities after v0.15: dated Commerce matchmaking episodes; primary program-to-office assignments; legacy award post-notice actions and authenticated agreements; country-level delivery confirmation. These are research priorities, not prerequisites for publishing the informative paper.
